## Invite members

From **Team** in the dashboard, invite new members by email. They'll receive an invitation to join your account.

## Roles

| Role | Manage members | Manage settings | Manage API keys | Write projects | Delete account |
|------|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Owner** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Admin** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Member** | No | No | No | Yes | No |
| **Viewer** | No | No | No | No | No |

## Change a member's role

Admins and owners can change roles from the team page. You cannot change your own role or promote someone above your own role level.

## Remove a member

Admins and owners can remove members. The owner cannot be removed — ownership must be transferred first.

## Audit log

All member changes (invites, role changes, removals) are logged in the **Audit Log**. Filter by action type to review specific events.
