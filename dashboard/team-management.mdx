Grantiva accounts are organizations. Every person with access holds a membership in the organization, and that membership carries a role that decides what they can do.

## Roles

There are four roles, ordered `viewer` < `member` < `admin` < `owner`. Permissions are cumulative — each role can do everything the one below it can.

| Role | Manage members | Manage settings | Manage API keys | Write projects | Delete organization |
|------|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Owner** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Admin** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Member** | No | No | No | Yes | No |
| **Viewer** | No | No | No | No | No |

- **Manage members** — invite, remove, and change roles
- **Manage settings** — rename the organization, change the billing email, edit notification preferences
- **Manage API keys** — create, rotate, and revoke [organization API keys](/api-reference/org/api-keys)
- **Write projects** — register apps, edit feature flags, custom claims, and webhooks
- **Delete organization** — owner only, and only once every other member has been removed

Viewers can still read everything: the member list, pending invites, API key metadata, and analytics.

## Seats

Every member and every **pending invite** consumes a seat. The limit comes from your plan:

| Plan | Seats |
|------|------:|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Business | 10 |
| Enterprise | 50 |

An invite that would push members plus pending invites past the limit is rejected with a message naming your current counts. Remove a member to free a seat, or [upgrade your plan](/dashboard/billing).

<Note>
A pending invite keeps holding its seat until it is accepted. There is currently no way to cancel one, so on a small plan invite deliberately.
</Note>

## Invite a teammate

Admins and owners can invite. From **Team** in the dashboard, enter the teammate's email address and pick the role they should get — **Member** if you don't choose.

The invite is rejected if:

- the email already belongs to a member of the organization
- a pending invite already exists for that email
- the organization is out of seats

Otherwise Grantiva emails the address a one-click acceptance link and the invite appears in the pending list.

### The invite flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite sent">
    The invite is created with a random single-use token and a **7-day expiry**, and an email goes to the invitee with an accept link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invitee signs in">
    They open the link and sign in to Grantiva — creating an account first if they don't have one. The account's email must match the address the invite was sent to; an invite accepted from a different email is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Membership created">
    Accepting adds them to the organization with the role the invite carried and marks the invite accepted. Accepting twice is harmless — an existing membership is left as it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inviter notified">
    If the organization's `teamInvite` [notification preference](/api-reference/org/settings) is on, the person who sent the invite gets an email confirming it was accepted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invite that sits unaccepted for 7 days expires and can no longer be used. It still counts against your seats, and a second invite to the same address is refused while the first is outstanding.

## Change a member's role

Admins and owners can change roles from the members page. Two rules apply:

- You cannot assign a role **higher than your own**. An admin can promote someone to admin, not to owner.
- Only an owner can change another **owner's** role.

Every change is recorded in the audit log as `member.role_changed`, with the old and new roles.

## Remove a member

Admins and owners can remove members, subject to two rules:

- You cannot remove **yourself**. Someone holding a higher role has to do it.
- You can only remove someone with a **strictly lower** role than yours. An admin cannot remove another admin, and an owner cannot be removed by anyone.

Removal deletes the membership. The person's Grantiva account continues to exist; they simply lose access to this organization. Any API keys they created belong to the organization and keep working — [revoke them](/api-reference/org/api-keys) separately if that matters.

<Note>
An owner can never be removed directly, because removal requires the target to hold a strictly lower role and nothing outranks owner. To hand the organization over: promote the new person to owner, have them demote you to admin, then have them remove you.
</Note>

## Audit log

Team changes are written to the organization audit log with a `resource.verb` action name:

| Action | Recorded when |
|--------|---------------|
| `invite.created` | An invite is sent — includes the email and role |
| `invite.accepted` | An invitee joins the organization |
| `member.role_changed` | A member's role changes — includes old and new role |
| `member.removed` | A member is removed — includes their email |

Read and filter the log from the dashboard, or through the [audit log API](/api-reference/org/settings).
